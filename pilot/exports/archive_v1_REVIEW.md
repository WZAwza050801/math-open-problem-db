## 2511.06207 — Mean Li-Yorke chaos for a sequence of operators on Banach spaces
journal_ref: `Acta Math. Sci. Ser. B (Engl. Ed.) 46 (2026), no. 3, 1187--1207`

### 🔴 OP-PILOT-0006 [not_real_open] MSC:47A16 难度:hard
**出处**：Section 1 (Introduction), paragraph reviewing Bernardes–Bonilla–Peris [BBP2020] on mean Li-Yorke chaos of operators on Banach spaces
**原文引文**：Some examples are are provided in  \cite{BBP2020} that there exists distributionally chaotic operators that are not mean Li-Yorke chaotic, and it still open whether the converse implication holds (see Question 16 in \cite{BBP2020}).
**自包含改写**：Let $T$ be a bounded linear operator on a Banach space $X$. $T$ is called mean Li-Yorke chaotic if there is an uncountable set $S\subset X$ such that every pair of distinct $x,y\in S$ satisfies $\liminf_{n\to\infty}\frac{1}{n}\sum_{i=1}^{n}\|T^i x - T^i y\| = 0$ and $\limsup_{n\to\infty}\frac{1}{n}\sum_{i=1}^{n}\|T^i x - T^i y\| > 0$. $T$ is distributionally chaotic if there is an uncountable set $S$ such that each distinct pair $x,y\in S$ is a distributionally chaotic pair, i.e. $F_{xy}(t) := \limsup_{n\to\infty}\frac{1}{n}|\{1\le i\le n: \|T^i x - T^i y\| < t\}| = 1$ for all $t>0$, while $\liminf_{n\to\infty}\frac{1}{n}|\{1\le i\le n: \|T^i x - T^i y\| < t_0\}| = 0$ for some $t_0>0$. Examples are known of operators that are distributionally chaotic but not mean Li-Yorke chaotic; the open question (Question 16 of Bernardes–Bonilla–Peris, J. Funct. Anal. 2020) is: must a mean Li-Yorke chaotic operator on a Banach space be distributionally chaotic?
**判定理由**：Merely cited background from another paper (Question 16 of [BBP2020]); this paper neither poses it as its own target nor addresses/resolves it for single operators or sequences.
**存疑**：The question is genuinely unresolved in the literature and is explicitly flagged as still open in the text, so under a stricter reading ('any explicitly stated open question regardless of origin') it could be relabeled real_open; however, within this paper it functions only as motivation and is not investigated. Exact wording of Question 16 in [BBP2020] was inferred from the quoted context.

## 2006.04987 — Langevin dynamic for the 2D Yang-Mills measure

### 🟢 OP-PILOT-0012 [real_open] MSC:60H15 难度:frontier

**原文引文**：It is natural to conjecture that the Markov process constructed in this paper possesses a unique invariant measure, for which the associated stochastic process indexed by Wilson loops agrees with the YM measure constructed in~\cite{Sengupta97, Levy03,Levy06}.
**自包含改写**：Let G be a compact Lie group with Lie algebra g, and consider the stochastic Yang-Mills (SYM) equation with DeTurck-Zwanziger gauge fixing on $\R_+ \times T^2$ ($T^2$ the 2-torus): $\partial_t A_i = \Delta A_i + \xi_i + \sum_{j=1}^2 [A_j, 2\partial_j A_i - \partial_i A_j + [A_j, A_i]]$, $i=1,2$, where $A$ is a $g$-valued 1-form (connection) and $\xi_1, \xi_2$ are i.i.d. $g$-valued space-time white noises; formally this is the Langevin (Parisi-Wu) dynamic for the Yang-Mills measure $\mu_{YM}(A) \propto \exp(-\int_{T^2}|F_A|^2 dx)\,dA$ with $F_A$ the curvature. **判定理由**：The paper constructs the orbit-valued Markov process but proves neither existence nor uniqueness of an invariant measure nor its identification with the Sengupta-Lévy 2D YM measure; explicitly stated as a conjecture.
**存疑**：A positive resolution would also solve the Section 1.2 question (see the separate entry on extracting a probability measure on the orbit space) and is one of the few rigorous links between the YM measure and the YM energy functional.
